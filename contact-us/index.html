Message us, call us, or email us. Here's which one to use when.

Use this when —

Make your first message count

The WhatsApp message that gets you a firm quote in one reply

Most people send "hi, do you alter suits?" and wait. That first message doesn't get you anywhere — I still need to ask four questions before I can quote. Here's the message that gets you a price on the first reply.

Copy this template and fill in the blanks

Hi David,

Garment: [e.g. men's navy wool suit jacket]
Issue: [e.g. sleeves are about 3cm too long, and I want the waist taken in slightly]
Location: [e.g. Kilimani]
Deadline: [e.g. before Friday 12th, or "no rush"]
Photo: [attach one front photo, one back photo, and a close-up of the sleeve]

Why this works: In one message, I know what you've got, what you need, where you are, and when you need it back. I can usually reply with a firm price — no back-and-forth. If your garment is unusual (leather, beaded, vintage), send an extra close-up of the fabric and I'll tell you honestly what's possible.

If you're not sure what's wrong with the garment

Hi David,

Garment: [type]
Problem: [in plain words — "it feels baggy", "the sleeves drag", "it sits too low"]
What I'd like: [even if you're not sure — "I want it slimmer", "I want it to fit properly", "not sure"]
Location: [estate]
Photo: [attach 2-3 photos from different angles]

You don't need to know tailoring terms. "It feels baggy" is enough for me to figure out what needs doing. I'll tell you what I see and what I recommend, then you decide. That's how most of my first conversations go.
