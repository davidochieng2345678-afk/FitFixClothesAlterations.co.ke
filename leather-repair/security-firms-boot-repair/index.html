What Makes Security Firm Boot Repair Different

Security firms run boots harder than almost any other client group. A guard wears the same pair for twelve hour shifts through sun, rain, night cold, concrete floors and stairwells. The boots take the load of standing for most of the shift, they see rain on outdoor posts, they pick up dust on parking lots and they get kicked against doors, gates and vehicles. Within six to nine months most boots start to show the wear, and within twelve months a lot of them are no longer serviceable without repair.

Replacing the whole team's boots every year is expensive and disruptive. Repairing them properly on a scheduled contract is faster, cheaper and keeps the team looking uniform on every shift. That is the model we built the security firm boot repair contract around at FitFix.

Every pair on a security firm contract is assessed for the load it takes on shift, the condition of the protective toe cap if any, the state of the sole and the heel, and the quality of the leather upper. The work is done in batches on a schedule that keeps officers in boots while the current batch is repaired. Why Security Firm Boot Contracts Work Better Than Buying New

Security firms in Nairobi are used to replacing guard boots every year. The maths of a scheduled repair contract usually beats that on every line.

A full security boot service package at KES 2,400 per pair keeps a good pair of boots in service for another full year of shifts. When boots are out of service, guards are either standing in shoes that are not compliant or sitting at the control room. A scheduled security contract keeps officers in boots through the year because it repairs in batches rather than losing the whole issue in one go.

A security firm with three hundred pairs on duty needs the boots to look the same across shifts. Every pair on a FitFix contract is cleaned, polished and finished to the same standard with matching stitch, sole and heel work, so the uniform standard holds on every shift and in every photograph.

Predictable Budget

Per pair pricing, a fixed schedule and one monthly invoice turn footwear into a maintenance line in the budget rather than an unpredictable capital cost. Finance teams can plan the year instead of reacting to a broken boot.

Safety Compliance Holds

Safety toe security boots that are half worn or half broken are a compliance risk on any site. A scheduled security contract keeps boots in a compliant state, and the protective toe cap and midsole are never touched during the repair.

Client Site Standards Are Met

Many corporate sites, banks, embassies and hotels that contract security firms have dress and safety standards for guards on their premises. A scheduled boot repair contract keeps the team looking correct and safe across all of those sites through the year.
