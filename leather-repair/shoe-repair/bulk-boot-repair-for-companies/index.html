Why Bulk Boot Contracts Work Better Than Buying New

Companies in Nairobi are used to replacing staff footwear every year. The maths of a bulk repair contract usually beats that on every line.

Cost Per Pair Per Year Falls

A bulk boot service package at KES 2,400 per pair keeps a good pair of boots in service for another year. That is a fraction of what a comparable new pair costs, and the company keeps the fit, the size and the sizing of the team rather than re issuing boots across the team every year.

No Stock-Out Risk

When boots are out of service, staff are either standing idle or wearing something unsuitable. A scheduled bulk contract keeps the team in boots through the year because it repairs in batches rather than losing the whole issue in one go.

Predictable Budget

Per pair pricing, a fixed schedule and one monthly invoice turn footwear into a maintenance line in the budget rather than an unpredictable capital cost. Finance teams can plan the year instead of reacting to a broken boot.

Uniform Look Across the Team

A security firm with three hundred pairs on duty needs the boots to look the same across shifts. Bulk repair keeps stitching, soles and finish consistent across the team so the uniform standard holds.

Safety Compliance Holds

Safety boots that are half worn or half broken are a compliance risk on site. A scheduled contract keeps boots in a compliant state, and the protective toe cap and midsole are never touched during the repair.

Fewer Replacements, Less Waste

Repairing boots keeps them out of landfill and reduces the number of new pairs purchased across the year. For companies with a sustainability or cost reduction target, that is a measurable line.
