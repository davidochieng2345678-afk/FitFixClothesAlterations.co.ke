The Mistake Most People Make

The single most common thing we see is a jacket that has been washed. Water and household soap strip the oils straight out of the leather. The jacket comes out of the wash looking brighter for a week, then goes stiff, then starts cracking at the flex points. By the time it reaches us the damage is often already there.

Leather does not behave like fabric. It needs oils put back in, not taken out. That is what conditioning is. On a well looked after jacket that is a light clean and a light feed twice a year. On a jacket that has been in a cupboard for three years, it is a deep clean, a mould check, a long slow condition and a protective finish on top. Two different jobs, and we quote them differently.

David Ochieng has cleaned and conditioned jackets in Nairobi since 2011, from daily-wear bikers to inherited coats that had not been touched in a decade. He looks at every jacket before he quotes because the right answer depends entirely on how dry the leather has gone.

How do I know if my leather jacket needs conditioning?

If the leather feels stiff, dry or papery, or it has started to crack at the flex points, it needs conditioning. If it has been in a cupboard for more than six months without being touched, it needs conditioning too. Send a photo and we tell you what it needs.

How often should a leather jacket be cleaned and conditioned in Nairobi?

Twice a year is the minimum for a jacket worn regularly. Once a year for a jacket worn occasionally. Nairobi sun, dust and rain pull the oils out of leather faster than most people realise, and conditioning twice a year is what stops it cracking.

Can a leather jacket that smells musty be cleaned in Nairobi?

Can a leather jacket be washed with soap and water?

No. Water and household soap strip the natural oils out of leather and leave it stiff and prone to cracking. Leather is cleaned with leather safe cleaners and conditioned with products made for leather. That is why we do not recommend DIY washing.

Can you clean the lining inside a leather jacket in Nairobi?

Can you clean a leather jacket that has been stored in a plastic bag for years?

Yes, most of the time. Storage in plastic traps moisture against the leather and grows mould. We clean, treat and condition the jacket and tell you honestly whether the leather itself has been damaged beyond repair.
