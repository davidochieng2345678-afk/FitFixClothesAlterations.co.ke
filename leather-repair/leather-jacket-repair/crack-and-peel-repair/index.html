What Makes Crack and Peel Repair Different

Crack and peel damage is the most misunderstood leather repair in the industry. A lot of people have been told their jacket is finished because it has started to peel at the shoulders or crack across the elbows. That is often not true. On most jackets that have cracked or peeled, the top finish layer has failed but the leather underneath is still structurally sound. That means the panel can be repaired by stabilising the crack, filling it, blending the colour and refinishing the surface so the damage disappears into the surrounding leather.

What makes crack and peel repair difficult is that the leather underneath has to be healthy for the repair to hold. A cracked surface over dead leather will crack again within weeks no matter how well the panel is filled. That is why every jacket is assessed panel by panel before any work starts, and why we tell you honestly when the leather underneath has failed and the panel is not worth repairing.

Why Leather Jackets Crack and Peel Faster in Nairobi

Nairobi is not a harsh desert and it is not a tropical rainforest, but the combination of the two destroys leather jacket finishes faster than most owners realise.

Strong UV at Altitude

Nairobi sits at altitude with strong midday sun for most of the year. Ultraviolet light breaks down the oils inside the finish layer and the leather underneath it. Once the finish has lost its oils, the surface starts to crack along the flex points and the shoulders. The cracks then lift the finish away from the leather, which is what shows up as peeling. This is the single biggest cause of crack and peel damage we see on jackets in Nairobi.

Repeated Wetting and Drying

The April, May, November and December rains soak jackets through, then the sun dries them fast. This cycle pulls the top finish away from the leather underneath because the two dry at different rates. Once the finish has separated from the leather, it peels. A rain exposed jacket that has never been conditioned is the most common crack and peel case we handle.

Dry Season Air

Nairobi's dry months pull moisture out of stored leather and dry the finish faster than the leather can reabsorb it. Dry leather does not flex cleanly at the shoulders and elbows, so the surface cracks along the flex lines. Conditioning twice a year is the single best way to prevent that cycle.

Storage in Dry or Damp Conditions

A jacket stored in a dry cupboard through a dry season goes brittle at the surface. A jacket stored in a plastic bag traps moisture against the leather and grows mould that weakens the finish. Both ends of that spectrum produce crack and peel damage within a season.

Bad Previous Repairs

A significant portion of our crack and peel work involves undoing previous repairs. Surface paint that was applied instead of penetrating dye and cracked within months. Filler that was too hard so the panel cracked at the edges of the repair. Colour that did not match the surrounding leather so the repair looked worse than the crack. Correcting these costs more than doing the job properly the first time, which is exactly why we do not cut corners.

Age of the Original Finish

Every leather finish has an end of life just like the leather itself. When the finish has thinned to the point where the base leather is showing through, or the panels are cracking in a network across the whole jacket, no amount of touching up will bring it back. That is the point where a full redye becomes the correct repair, and we will tell you honestly whether your jacket has reached it.
