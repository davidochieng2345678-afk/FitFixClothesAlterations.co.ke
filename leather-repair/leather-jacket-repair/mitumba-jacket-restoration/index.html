Why Nairobi Is the Best Place to Buy Mitumba Leather

Nairobi has one of the deepest second hand leather markets in East Africa. The jackets that come through Gikomba, Toi, Ngara and the various mtumba yards are the same hides that were sold new in Milan, London and Tokyo a decade or two ago, at a small fraction of what they cost then. A well chosen mitumba leather jacket is often better than anything new at triple the price.

The catch is that these jackets have usually been worn hard before they reached the stall. The zip has been opened and closed a few thousand times. The lining has taken years of sweat against the armpits. The shoulders have seen sun through car windows. By the time the jacket reaches you, the leather hide is usually still fine but everything around it is tired. That is exactly what restoration is for.

David Ochieng has restored hundreds of mitumba leather jackets in Nairobi since 2011. He has seen every failure pattern these jackets come with: the collar that has gone shiny from being worn over a shirt, the zip tape that has dried out, the lining that has disintegrated at the back seam, the shoulders that have cracked in a fine network. He also knows the ones worth buying and restoring and the ones that should stay at the stall. When you send photos he will tell you which one you have.
