Materials That Hold Up in Nairobi Conditions

  • Bonded nylon and waxed polyester thread. Holds tension, resists abrasion, does not rot like cotton when it gets wet.
  • Footwear grade contact adhesives. Formulated to stay flexible through heat and cold, not the brittle all purpose glue that cracks within months.
  • Penetrating leather dyes. Go into the hide and move with it, unlike surface paints that crack the first time the leather flexes.
  • Matched hardware. Zips, sliders, buckles, D rings, rivets, snap fasteners and clasps in brass, nickel, gunmetal and antique finishes, matched to what the item already carries.
  • Conditioners and protective finishes. Feed the leather and add a water resistant layer without leaving a greasy film that attracts dust.
  • Edge paints and burnishing waxes. Seal cut edges so they stop absorbing dirt and water and stop darkening unevenly.
