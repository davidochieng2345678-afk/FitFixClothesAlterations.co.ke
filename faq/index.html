Booking and Pickup

How do I book a service with FitFix in Nairobi?

Send two photos of your item to WhatsApp 0705 728 896, one of the whole item and one close up of the area that needs work, along with your location in Nairobi. David replies with a firm price, a turnaround and a free pickup window. No deposit, no inspection fee, pay on delivery by M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer. If you would rather call, the same number reaches us by voice.

Do you offer free pickup and delivery in Nairobi?

Yes. Pickup and delivery is completely free across the Nairobi ring, including Westlands, Karen, Kilimani, Lavington, Kileleshwa, Parklands, Riverside, Langata, South B, South C, Embakasi, Kasarani, Ruiru, Kiambu Road, Ngong, Rongai, Kitengela, Athi River and Syokimau. There is no minimum order value, no collection fee and no delivery fee. Outside the Nairobi ring we repair and ship by courier at your cost.

Can I drop items off in person instead of booking a pickup?

Yes. Our workshop is in Industrial Area, Nairobi, P.O. Box 6262-00100 and clients are welcome to drop items off in person during opening hours, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. WhatsApp 0705 728 896 first so we can confirm we are on the bench and not out on a collection round. In-person drop-off and pickup is a good option if you would rather meet David in person, or if you want to discuss a specific job before leaving the item.

What do you need in the photos I send on WhatsApp?

Two photos: one of the whole item laid flat or held up, and one close up of the area that needs work. Both in daylight, not flash at night. For shoes, add one of the sole and one of the side. For leather jackets, add one of the zip and one of the lining. For bridal or formal wear, add one of the dress on the wearer and one close up of the area that needs work. The more specific the photos, the more accurate the price and turnaround we return.

Can you collect from an office reception desk?

Yes. Many FitFix pickups are from office reception desks in the CBD, Upper Hill, Westlands, Parklands, Kilimani and Industrial Area. Leave the item with reception and send us the receptionist's name and the building details, and the rider collects without needing to disturb your workday. A receipt is issued on the spot and the item is photographed at collection.

Can you collect from a gate or estate security desk?

Yes. Many FitFix pickups are from estate gates, especially where access is restricted or where the client is not at home. Leave the item with the gate or security desk, send us the estate name, gate number and the name to ask for, and the rider collects and issues a receipt on the spot.

What happens if I am not at home when the rider arrives?

The rider waits for a short while and calls you. If you cannot be reached, we reschedule for the next available window at no extra cost. If you have arranged a gate or reception drop-off, the rider collects from the agreed point and issues a receipt on the spot without needing to see you.

Do I have to pay anything at pickup?

No. There is no deposit, no inspection fee and no collection fee. You pay the full amount on delivery once the finished item is back in your hands, by M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer. If you cancel after collection for any reason, we return the item to you at no cost.

Can I book a specific pickup time in advance?

Yes. You can book a specific date and time up to two weeks ahead, which is useful before weddings, trips, events and busy working weeks. WhatsApp 0705 728 896 with your preferred window and we confirm the booking in writing on WhatsApp before the rider is dispatched.

Can multiple items be collected on one pickup?

Yes. Multiple items from the same household or office are collected on one pickup, on one invoice and returned on one delivery. There is no limit on the number of items on a standard pickup, though for very large batches (over 40 items) we ask for advance notice so we can plan the route and the box space properly.

Pricing and Payment

How do I pay for a FitFix job?

M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer. There is no deposit required on any alteration, repair or restoration job and no inspection fee. You pay in full on delivery once the finished item is back in your hands. For diplomatic and expatriate clients we can settle invoices in USD, GBP or EUR by bank transfer on request. For corporate clients, invoiced terms of 14 days or 30 days are available from the second batch onwards.

Can I get a firm price before you collect?

Yes. Send two photos to WhatsApp 0705 728 896 and we reply with the firm price, the turnaround and the pickup window before a rider is dispatched. The price you are quoted is the price you pay, unless we find a hidden problem on collection that changes the work required, in which case we call before starting and either revise the price with your agreement or return the item untouched and free of charge.

Why are your prices higher or lower than other Nairobi tailors?

Our prices are set to reflect the actual work each job requires, the materials we use and the fourteen years of experience on the bench. Some Nairobi tailors charge less because they use cheaper thread, cheaper hardware or thinner linings, and those jobs usually fail within a season. Some charge more because they are targeting a specific market with a retail overhead we do not have. We publish starting prices openly on our pricing page so you can see where any specific job sits before you contact us.

Do you charge extra for pickup and delivery?

No. Pickup and delivery is completely free across the Nairobi ring. There is no collection fee, no delivery fee, no fuel surcharge and no minimum order value. The free pickup is built into the price of the work you book.

What if the price changes after you see the item?

The price you are quoted on photo is the price you pay in 95 percent of cases. Where the item on collection differs from the photo in a way that changes the work required — for example, hidden damage under a lining, or a repair that was not visible in the photo — we call you before starting and either revise the price with your agreement or return the item untouched and free of charge. We never add cost without your explicit agreement.

Do you offer discounts on multiple items or bulk bookings?

Yes for corporate and bulk bookings. Corporate per-piece pricing applies from 10 pieces on a single batch for most services and from 50 pieces for school uniform programmes. For household batches of two or three items, standard pricing applies because the work itself does not change. For a full wedding party, a bridal programme or a corporate batch, ask for a group quote on WhatsApp 0705 728 896 and we will confirm what pricing is available.

Can I pay in installments or by a deposit?

No. FitFix does not take deposits and does not offer installment payments on individual jobs. Payment is in full on delivery. For corporate clients on invoiced terms, the first batch is payable on delivery and invoiced terms are available from the second batch onwards.

Can I pay by card or PayPal?

M-Pesa, cash, KES bank transfer and, for diplomatic and expatriate clients, USD, GBP or EUR bank transfer are the accepted payment methods. We do not accept card payments on delivery because our riders do not carry card terminals. PayPal is not a supported payment method.

Do you charge a cancellation fee?

No. If you cancel after collection for any reason, we return the item to you at no cost. If you cancel before collection, there is no charge at all. There is no cancellation fee on any FitFix booking.

Is there a fee for the first inspection or assessment?

No. The photo assessment that produces your firm quote is free, and the in-person assessment on collection is free. There is no inspection fee at any stage of a FitFix booking.

Timing and Turnaround

How long does a FitFix job take?

Hem and small repair jobs take one to two working days from collection. Suit alterations take three to five working days. Leather jacket repair takes two to five working days depending on the work. Shoe repair takes two to three working days. Bridal and wedding dress alterations run five to ten working days on the standard programme. Full leather restorations take three to five working days. All timings run from the day the item is collected, not from the day you send the photo. Rush turnaround is available where capacity allows and is quoted at booking.

What is your turnaround for a rush job in Nairobi?

Rush turnaround is available on some services where capacity allows and is quoted at the time of booking. Same day or 24-hour turnaround is possible for simple jobs such as a single trouser hem, a strap adjustment, a shoe polish or a small wallet repair. Rush turnaround on larger jobs such as a full suit rebuild, a bridal bodice rebuild or a leather jacket reline is usually not possible and we will tell you that honestly before you commit.

How long does bridal work take from the wedding date?

The standard bridal programme runs six to eight weeks ahead of the wedding date, with a first fitting ten to fourteen days after the first pickup, a second fitting about ten days later, and a final pickup about five days before the wedding. A four-week compressed programme is possible where capacity allows. Two weeks is only enough for simple alterations such as straps or a small hem adjustment. Bridal work is scheduled against the wedding date at booking and the schedule is confirmed in writing.

How long does a corporate batch take?

Small corporate batches of 10 to 20 pieces take three to five working days. Medium batches of 20 to 60 pieces take five to seven working days. Large batches of 60 to 200 pieces take seven to twelve working days. Recurring contracts on monthly or quarterly pickups run on a fixed schedule agreed with the client.

Can you rush a job if I have an event on a specific date?

Yes, where capacity allows and where the work is simple enough for the timeframe. Tell us your event date when you send the photo and we will confirm what is possible before you commit. If the timeframe is not achievable for the work involved, we will tell you honestly rather than promising something we cannot deliver.

What happens if a job takes longer than the turnaround I was quoted?

If a job runs longer than the turnaround you were quoted, we contact you on WhatsApp before the original return date and confirm the revised date. In the rare cases where the delay is caused by us and not by a change in the work, we complete the job at the price you were quoted without adding any waiting-related charges. There is no penalty fee either way.

Do you work on weekends or public holidays?

FitFix works Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Pickups and deliveries run within those hours. If a public holiday falls on a working day, that day is skipped and the schedule shifts to the next working day.

Can you hold an item until a specific date for a delivery?

Yes. If you have a wedding, event or trip and would rather not receive the finished item until a specific date, tell us at booking and we will hold the item at the workshop until the date you request. There is no storage charge for holding a completed item for up to two weeks.

How quickly do you reply on WhatsApp during opening hours?

Most WhatsApp messages are answered within the hour during opening hours, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm. First-time enquiries that include photos are answered fastest because we can quote straight away. Messages that arrive after 6pm are answered the next working morning.

Tailoring and Alterations

Suits, blazers, casual jackets, trench coats, overcoats and waistcoats. Bridal gowns, bridesmaid dresses, prom dresses, formal wear and school uniforms. Garment repairs including re-lining, darning, strap adjustment, elastic replacement, zipper replacement and fabric patching. Kitenge and African wear alterations. Suit alterations start from KES 1,500 for jacket sleeve shortening and go up to KES 6,500 for a full suit rebuild. Jacket waist suppression starts from KES 2,500. Shoulder reduction starts from KES 5,500. Jacket lengthening starts from KES 4,500. Trouser hemming within a suit starts from KES 400. Can you alter a suit that is too big?

Yes, and it is one of the most common jobs we take on. A suit that is one size too big can usually be taken in at the waist, the jacket body and the trouser seat. Shoulders can be reduced. Sleeves can be shortened. A suit that is two sizes too big can still be reduced but requires more work and may need the jacket rebuilt at the shoulder to sit correctly. Where a suit is genuinely past the point of a clean alteration, we tell you on photo before starting.

Can you alter a suit that is too small?

Sometimes. A suit that is one size too small can sometimes be let out at the side seams and the trouser waist if there is enough seam allowance in the garment. A suit that is two sizes too small usually cannot be let out because there is simply not enough fabric in the seams. We tell you honestly on photo what is and is not possible and do not start work that will not hold.

Can you shorten suit sleeves that have functional buttons?

Yes. Suit sleeves with functional cuff buttons (a surgeon's cuff) are shortened by opening the sleeve at the shoulder rather than the cuff, so the functional buttons stay functional and the sleeve sits correctly at the wrist. This is a more involved job than shortening a plain cuff but it is standard work on the bench.

Can you alter suit shoulder width?

Yes. Shoulder reduction starts from KES 5,500 and involves removing the sleeve, reducing the shoulder width, resetting the sleeve head and finishing the armhole so the shoulder sits cleanly. A shoulder can be reduced but cannot be expanded, so a suit with shoulders that are too narrow cannot be altered. We tell you on photo whether the shoulders can be reduced by the amount you need.

Do you alter school uniforms?

Yes. School uniform alterations start from KES 300 per piece and cover shirts, trousers, skirts, tunics, blazers, sports kits and ties. Family batches of two or more children's uniforms are collected on one pickup and returned on one delivery. Bulk school uniform programmes for schools and parents groups start from KES 250 per piece on orders of 50 pieces or more. See the school uniform alterations page for full detail.

Do you alter kitenge and African wear?

Yes. Kitenge, dashiki, leso and kanga alterations are handled at the workshop with matched thread and fabric finish. Kitenge alterations are quoted on photo before pickup, and we take care to preserve the original print where the alteration crosses a patterned section. See the kitenge and African wear alterations page for full detail.

Can you replace a zip in a garment?

Yes. Zipper replacement in garments starts from KES 700 and covers trouser zips, jacket zips, dress zips and bag zips. The failed zip is removed, a matching metal or nylon zip is fitted, and the opening is restitched so the slider runs cleanly. Where the zip is a decorative or specific finish, we source a matching replacement wherever possible.

Can you re-line a jacket or coat?

Yes. Re-lining starts from KES 1,500 and covers suit jackets, blazers, casual jackets, overcoats, trench coats and leather jackets. The failed lining is removed, a new lining in matching or upgraded fabric is cut and fitted, and the lining is reseated at the neckline, sleeve and back so it does not show through the outer garment.

Do you do visible mending or invisible mending?

Both, depending on the fabric and the damage. Invisible mending is the default on formalwear, where the repair is blended into the surrounding weave so it does not stand out. Visible mending is a deliberate style choice where the repair becomes part of the garment, useful on denim, workwear and casual pieces. Darning and invisible mending starts from KES 500. Tell us on photo what outcome you want.

Can you alter a dress with sequins or beading?

Yes. Dresses with sequins or beading are altered by removing the decorative layer by hand at the seam line, opening the underlying seam, reshaping, and reapplying the decoration so the pattern continues correctly. This is slower than altering a plain dress and is the reason a sequined hem costs more than a plain one. The starting price depends on the density of the decoration and is quoted on photo.

What about V-joint crotch adjustment on trousers?

V-joint crotch adjustment starts from KES 1,200 and covers repairing or reshaping the V-joint at the crotch of trousers, reinforcing the area and finishing the seams so the trouser sits cleanly at the seat. This is one of the most requested specialist alterations in Nairobi and one of the hardest jobs to do correctly because the crotch seam sits under constant tension. See the V-joint crotch adjustment page for full detail.

Can you make a trouser waist smaller or larger?

Yes. Trouser waist adjustment starts from KES 800 and covers both reduction and expansion. A trouser waist can typically be reduced by several centimetres and expanded by one or two centimetres depending on the seam allowance in the garment. Where expansion beyond the available seam allowance is needed, we tell you honestly and discuss alternatives such as adding matching fabric inserts at the side seams.

How many fittings does a bridal alteration need?

Most brides need two fittings. The first fitting happens ten to fourteen days after the first pickup, when the main adjustments have been tacked in place. The second fitting happens about ten days later when the final adjustments have been made and the gown is ready for a final check. Full bodice rebuilds and corset installations sometimes need a third fitting. Rush programmes inside three weeks may compress to one fitting plus a final pickup.

Do you preserve lace and beading when altering a wedding dress?

Yes. Preserving lace, beading and embroidery is the single most important part of wedding dress alteration. Every gown is opened along the least visible seams, the lace and beading are removed and resecured by hand where necessary, and the alteration is closed so the original decoration sits correctly over the new seam. Where the beading or lace is fragile, it is reinforced underneath before the seam is closed. Heavy beading takes longer and is quoted on photo.

Can you install a corset in a wedding dress?

Yes. Corset installation starts from KES 12,000 and covers a full internal corset with boning, a lace-up back where required and a matching inner lining so the corset does not show through the gown. This is one of the most common bridal alterations in Nairobi because many wedding dresses are bought with only a zip back and would sit more securely on the bride with a lace-up corset.

Can you add a bustle to a wedding dress train?

Yes. Bustle construction starts from KES 4,500 and adds two to five concealed hooks or ribbons depending on the weight and length of the train. The bustle is fitted and tested at the second fitting so the bride and her maid of honour know exactly how to lift the train at the reception. Almost every bride with a train longer than half a metre needs a bustle.

Can you repair a leather jacket that another workshop has already tried to fix?

Often yes. A failed previous repair is opened, cleaned, corrected and rebuilt where the leather underneath is still sound. Can you repair a leather jacket zip that has failed at the corners?

Yes. Jacket zip replacement starts from KES 1,000 and covers the removal of the failed zip, fitting a matching metal or nylon zip and reseating the leather so the slider runs smoothly. On leather jackets the zip corners are the most common failure point because the leather has less give than fabric at the curve. Where the original hardware is a specific finish, we source a matching replacement.

Can you redye a faded leather jacket?

Yes. Redyeing and colour restoration starts from KES 2,800 and covers cleaning, prepping, redyeing in the original colour or a new colour and sealing so the finish holds. The achievable match depends on the original leather type and the amount of fading. Where the match is not going to be close, we tell you on photo before starting so there are no surprises.

Can you repair cracked or peeling leather?

Sometimes. Crack and peel repair starts from KES 2,000 and covers filling and refinishing cracked or peeling leather, matched in colour to the surrounding surface. Where the leather has gone fully dry and the cracks run through the leather rather than just the surface finish, a repair will not hold and we tell you honestly before starting. Where the surface has cracked but the leather underneath is still sound, the repair can be very effective.

Can you restore a mitumba jacket?

Can you restore the colour on faded or scuffed leather shoes?

Can you repair safety boots for a security firm or industrial company?

Can you clean and restore sneakers?

Can you repair the insole and lining of a leather shoe?

Wallets, Belts and Accessories

How much does wallet repair cost in Nairobi?

Wallet repair starts from KES 500 and covers reinforcing a split fold, restitching torn card slots, replacing a snap fastener and refinishing the edges in matched colour. Bifold and trifold wallets are both handled. See the wallet repair page for full detail.

Can you repair a wallet that has split along the fold?

Yes. A split fold is the most common wallet failure and one of the repairs we do most often. The leather along the fold is reinforced, the spine is restitched in bonded nylon, and the leather on both sides of the fold is cleaned, conditioned and refinished so the fold moves cleanly again rather than cracking along the same line. The result is a wallet that holds for several more years.

Can you resize a belt that is too long?

Yes. Belt resizing starts from KES 700 and covers cutting from the buckle end, refitting the buckle, punching a fresh set of prong holes and refinishing the cut edge so the belt looks factory finished rather than obviously altered. A belt can be shortened by several sizes. See the belt resizing page for full detail.

Can you lengthen a belt?

A belt cannot be invisibly lengthened because the leather is a single cut length. Where the belt has been cut short but has room at the buckle end, we can refit the buckle at the existing fold and add new holes so the belt can be worn further out. Where the belt has been cut past that point, the honest answer is a new belt and we tell you that before starting.

Can you replace a broken belt buckle?

Yes. Buckle replacement starts from KES 800 and covers the removal of the old buckle, fitting a matching brass, nickel, gunmetal or antique buckle and restitching the belt fold around the new buckle. Where the buckle finish needs to match other hardware on the belt such as rivets or eyelets, we match the plating tone wherever an exact match is available. See the belt buckle replacement page for full detail.

Can you repair a watch strap?

Yes. Watch strap repair starts from KES 700 and covers restitching, hole repunching, buckle replacement and full strap replacement in matching or contrasting leather. Where the strap has fully worn through, a replacement strap is cut and fitted to the watch case. See the leather accessories page for full detail.

Can you repair a card holder or passport holder?

Yes. Card holder and passport holder repair starts from KES 500 and covers restitching split slots, reinforcing card pockets, refinishing edges and replacing snap fasteners where needed. Card holders with torn slot stitching are one of the most common small leather goods repairs because the card edges wear the thread through over a few years of daily use.

Can you repair luggage tags and key pouches?

Yes. Luggage tag and key pouch repair starts from KES 500 and covers restitching straps and loops, replacing rivets and refinishing edges. Luggage tags often fail at the strap attachment point during travel, and key pouches often fail at the zip or the loop. Both are quick, straightforward repairs.

Can you restore a whole set of small leather goods in one pickup?

Yes. Full small leather goods restoration starts from KES 2,500 and covers a wallet, a belt and one additional item in one pass on one pickup. Larger sets — wallet, two belts, watch strap and card holder — are also welcome. Everything travels on one free pickup and returns on one delivery.

Do you repair belts and wallets for both men and women?

Yes. Men's and women's wallets, belts, card holders, watch straps and small leather goods are all repaired at the workshop with the same range of services. Pricing is the same because the work itself is the same.

Bespoke Tailoring

Do you do bespoke tailoring from scratch in Nairobi?

Yes. FitFix offers bespoke tailoring for shirts, trousers, waistcoats, two-piece suits, three-piece suits, overcoats and ceremonial wear. Bespoke projects start from KES 8,500 for a shirt and from KES 22,000 for a two-piece suit depending on the fabric chosen. Bespoke runs on a programme with a first measurement appointment, fabric selection, one or two fittings and final delivery. See the bespoke tailoring services page for full detail.

How does bespoke pricing work?

Bespoke pricing has two components: the tailoring cost and the fabric cost. The tailoring cost is a fixed starting figure for each garment, listed on the pricing page and the bespoke page. The fabric cost depends on the fabric you choose, which is quoted at the first appointment after you have selected it. There is no deposit required at the first appointment. Pricing is confirmed in writing before the work begins.

How many fittings does a bespoke suit need?

A bespoke two-piece or three-piece suit typically needs two fittings after the initial measurement appointment. The first fitting happens a few weeks after the fabric is cut, when the suit is basted together for checking. The second fitting happens a couple of weeks later when the final adjustments have been made. Simple bespoke garments such as shirts and trousers may need only one fitting.

How long does a bespoke suit take?

A bespoke two-piece suit typically takes six to ten weeks from first measurement to final delivery, depending on the fabric and the number of fittings needed. Bespoke shirts take three to five weeks. Bespoke trousers take four to six weeks. Bespoke overcoats and three-piece suits take longer. Rush bespoke programmes are possible where capacity allows and are quoted at the first appointment.

What fabrics do you offer for bespoke?

We offer a range of imported and local fabrics for bespoke work, from everyday business suiting to formal occasion fabrics. Fabric selection happens at the first appointment and the specific range available at any given time depends on what has been received in stock. Where a specific fabric is not available, we tell you honestly and offer alternatives.

Can I bring my own fabric for bespoke work?

Yes, in most cases. Where you have bought your own fabric, we assess it at the first appointment to confirm whether it is suitable for the garment you want, whether it has been pre-shrunk, and whether the yardage is sufficient. Where the fabric is not suitable for the intended garment, we tell you honestly before starting.

Do you do bespoke bridal or ceremonial wear?

Yes. Bespoke ceremonial wear for weddings, traditional functions and specific occasions is handled on the same programme as bespoke suiting. Bespoke ceremonial and traditional wear starts from KES 18,000 depending on the design and fabric. Where a specific cultural or traditional cut is required, we discuss the design at the first appointment and confirm what we can deliver before starting.

Can a bespoke suit be altered later if my measurements change?

Yes. A bespoke suit is built with enough seam allowance to allow for small measurement changes over time. Waist, sleeve, trouser hem and trouser waist adjustments are all straightforward on a bespoke garment. Shoulder and body changes are more involved but usually possible. A bespoke suit is one of the most alterable garments you can own.

Warranty and Quality

What happens if a job is not done correctly?

If a job is not done correctly, we fix it at no cost. Tell us on WhatsApp 0705 728 896 within 14 days of delivery, describe the problem and, where possible, send a photo. If the problem is caused by wear after delivery, use of the item in a way that was not appropriate for that garment, or a change in the wearer's measurements since the work was completed, we will still assess the item, tell you honestly what happened and quote a fair price to correct the issue if correction is possible. What does the FitFix warranty cover?

The warranty covers workmanship on any alteration, repair or restoration completed at FitFix. If a seam opens, a stitch fails, a zip breaks at a point away from the slider, a buckle comes loose from its fold, or a repair fails within 14 days of delivery, we correct it at no cost. The warranty does not cover damage caused by external factors such as heavy wear, exposure to chemicals, or the item being used in a way that was not appropriate for its original design.

How long is the FitFix warranty?

Fourteen days from the date of delivery for workmanship on all alterations, repairs and restorations. This is the standard warranty period on the site. Where a specific job carries a longer warranty — for example, certain resoling jobs and certain buckle replacement jobs — that will be confirmed in writing at the time of the job.

Do you offer a refund if a job is not done correctly?

FitFix does not offer refunds on completed alteration or repair work because the work itself cannot be returned once it has been done to a garment or leather good. If a job has not been done correctly, we fix it at no cost within the 14-day warranty period. If the item is beyond correction, we assess it individually and offer a fair resolution. See the returns and refund policy for full detail.

What if I am not happy with the fit on a garment alteration?

If the fit is not right, tell us within 14 days of delivery and send a photo of the garment on the wearer. Where the fit is off because of a mistake on our side, we correct it at no cost. Where the fit is off because of a small change in the wearer's measurements since the alteration was completed, or because the way the garment was worn on the day is different from how it was fitted, we still assess the item and tell you honestly what can be done and at what price. In most cases a small further adjustment resolves the issue.

Do you offer warranty on bridal alterations?

Yes. Bridal alterations are covered by the same 14-day workmanship warranty, counted from the final pickup of the gown. Because wedding dates are fixed, we also offer additional support in the immediate run-up to the wedding where a bride notices an issue — WhatsApp 0705 728 896 and we will arrange an emergency adjustment where capacity allows.

How do I make a warranty claim?

WhatsApp 0705 728 896 within 14 days of delivery with your name, the date of delivery, a description of the issue and, where possible, a photo of the problem. David assesses the claim and either confirms the correction or explains the position. Where the correction is covered by the warranty, we collect the item free and return it free once corrected.

Does the warranty cover normal wear and tear?

No. The warranty covers workmanship, not normal wear and tear. A trouser hem that frays after six months of daily wear is normal wear. A suit sleeve that pulls apart at the seam a week after delivery is workmanship and is covered. Where the boundary between the two is unclear, we assess the item and tell you honestly which side it falls on.
