# FitFix Clothes Alterations - Nairobi, Kenya

Welcome to the official GitHub repository for **FitFix Clothes Alterations**, a professional tailoring and garment alteration service based in Nairobi, Kenya. 

This repository contains the source code for our static website, which serves as our online booking hub and portfolio. We specialize in giving your clothes a perfect fit, from everyday wear to special occasions.

## ✂️ Our Services
We offer a wide range of professional alteration and repair services, including:
- **Suit & Blazer Alterations:** Resizing, sleeve shortening, shoulder reduction, and jacket lengthening.
- **Trouser & Jeans Alterations:** Hemming, waist adjustments, tapering, and crotch (V-joint) repairs.
- **Dress & Bridal Alterations:** Bridal wear, bridesmaid dresses, evening gowns, and traditional Kitenge wear.
- **Outerwear:** Leather jackets, overcoats, trenchcoats, and casual jackets.
- **Repairs & Mending:** Zipper replacement, re-lining, invisible mending, fabric patching, and elastic replacement.
- **School Uniforms:** Quick and reliable sizing adjustments for growing children.
