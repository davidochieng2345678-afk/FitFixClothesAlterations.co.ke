1. Who we are

FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke ("we", "us", "our") is a tailoring and garment alteration business based in Industrial Area, Nairobi, Kenya, operated by David Ochieng as sole proprietor. We provide clothing alteration services across Nairobi with free door-to-door pickup and delivery.

For the purposes of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we are the data controller for the personal information you provide to us. That means we are the party responsible for how your information is collected, used and stored.

Our business address is Industrial Area, Nairobi 00500, Kenya. Our contact details are in Section 15.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect the minimum information we need to alter your garments and deliver them back to you. Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Identity and contact details — your name, WhatsApp number, and (if you provide it) your email address.
  • Delivery information — the estate, building, office or residence where you would like us to collect and deliver your garments, and any access notes you share (for example "ask for me at reception").
  • Garment information — details of the garments you send to us: type of garment, fabric, colour, size, visible damage, and any prior alterations you tell us about.
  • Measurements — the body and garment measurements we take to complete your alteration. On repeat jobs, we keep these so we can match your fit.
  • Communication content — the messages, photos, voice notes and videos you send us over WhatsApp, email or SMS, and our replies to you.
  • Payment information — the amount paid and the payment reference (for example an M-Pesa confirmation code). We do not receive or store your full card number, M-Pesa PIN, or bank credentials.

2.2 Information we collect automatically when you use this website

  • Technical data — your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, and the pages you visit on this site.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the site (pages viewed, links clicked, time spent). This is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
  • Cookies — small text files stored on your device by your browser. See Section 9 for details.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect sensitive personal data as defined by the Kenya Data Protection Act (health data, religious beliefs, political opinions, genetic or biometric data, sexual orientation).
  • We do not collect ID numbers, KRA PINs, or copies of national identification documents.
  • We do not collect payment card details — all payments are made via M-Pesa, cash, or direct bank transfer, and confirmation is manual.

3. How we collect it

We collect your personal information in the following ways:

  • Directly from you — when you message us on WhatsApp, call us, send us an email, or fill in any contact form on this site.
  • During collection and delivery — when a rider picks up or returns your garments, or when you drop off or collect in person at our Industrial Area workshop.
  • During the alteration — when we take measurements and note garment details in our workshop records.
  • Automatically — via cookies and standard server logs when you browse this website.

4. Why we collect it — lawful basis

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. The bases we rely on are:

  • Performance of a contract — to alter your garments and deliver them back to you, which is the service you have asked us to provide.
  • Legitimate interest — to operate and improve our business, prevent fraud, keep basic records, and answer customer enquiries efficiently.
  • Consent — where you have explicitly agreed to something (for example, agreeing to receive occasional updates about the workshop). You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with Kenyan tax, business and other applicable legal requirements.

We only process your data for the purposes we describe in this policy. If we ever want to use your data for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it. Our retention periods are:

  • WhatsApp conversations and photos — kept for up to 24 months from the last message, so we can refer back if you bring a garment back for adjustment or a related job.
  • Garment measurements and alteration notes — kept for up to 36 months so we can match your fit on repeat jobs without re-measuring.
  • Name and contact details — kept for up to 36 months from your most recent job. If you have not used us in 36 months, we delete them.
  • Payment and accounting records — kept for 5 years as required by the Kenya Revenue Authority.
  • Website analytics — aggregated and anonymised data is kept for up to 14 months.
  • Cookies — see Section 9 for individual cookie lifespans.

If you ask us to delete your data, we will delete everything we are not legally required to keep — usually within 7 working days. Records we must keep for tax purposes (the payment records listed above) are retained until the legal retention period ends, then deleted automatically.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone. We share it only in the following limited situations:

  • Riders and courier partners — the motorcycle riders who collect and deliver your garments receive your first name, phone number, and pickup/delivery address. They do not receive your measurements, garment history, or payment details.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) — our primary communication channel is WhatsApp. Messages sent to and from our business number are handled by WhatsApp's own infrastructure under their privacy policy and their end-to-end encryption standard.
  • Our website host and email provider — the services that run this website and our contact email handle data on our behalf as processors. They are contractually required to protect it and to process it only on our instructions.
  • Professional advisers — accountants or legal advisers where strictly necessary, and only under confidentiality.
  • Legal requirement — where we are required by Kenyan law, court order or a regulatory authority (such as the KRA or ODPC) to disclose information.
  • With your explicit consent — if you ask us to share your details with a specific third party (for example, if you want a receipt sent to a family member).

We do not share your personal data with marketing companies, advertising networks, data brokers, or any other third party for their own purposes.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to keep your personal data safe:

  • Encryption in transit — this website is served over HTTPS with TLS encryption. Any data you submit through the site is encrypted in transit.
  • End-to-end encrypted messaging — WhatsApp conversations between you and our business number are protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption, which means the content is not readable by anyone in the middle, including WhatsApp itself.
  • Access control — only David Ochieng and, where strictly necessary, one trusted assistant have access to customer records. Each accesses only what they need to complete the job in front of them.
  • Device security — devices used to access customer records are protected with screen locks, operating system updates, and (where available) biometric access.
  • Physical security — paper records (measurement cards, alteration notes) are kept in a secured location at our Industrial Area workshop. Garments themselves are stored in a dedicated area and are not accessible to the public.
  • Regular review — we review our data practices at least once a year and whenever we change how we operate.

No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee that data will never be affected by a breach. If a breach ever affects your personal data in a way that puts you at risk, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as required under the Act.

8. Your rights under Kenyan law

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the following rights over your personal data. You can exercise any of them by contacting us using the details in Section 15. We will respond within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Right to be informed

You have the right to know what data we collect and how we use it. This policy is part of how we meet that right.

Right of access

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, along with an explanation of how we use it.

Right to correction

If any information we hold is wrong, out of date, or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it. We will do so promptly.

Right to erasure

You can ask us to delete your personal data. We will delete everything we are not legally required to keep (payment records, for example, must be retained for tax purposes).

Right to restrict processing

You can ask us to stop using your data for a specific purpose while still keeping it on file — for example, to stop sending you updates but keep your measurements.

Right to object

You can object to any processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interest. We will stop unless we have a compelling reason to continue.

Right to data portability

Where we process your data by automated means on the basis of contract or consent, you can ask us to give it to you in a structured, commonly-used format.

Right to withdraw consent

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before the withdrawal.

You can exercise any of these rights free of charge by messaging us on WhatsApp, calling, or emailing. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we action a request, so we do not disclose data to the wrong person.

9. Cookies and website data

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small text files that your browser stores on your device when you visit a website. We use them to:

  • Keep the site working properly — remembering basic settings, ensuring pages load correctly, and supporting core functions such as navigation.
  • Understand how the site is used — aggregated statistics on which pages are visited and how long visitors stay, so we can improve the content.
  • Remember your cookie preference — so you are not asked repeatedly.

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or tracking you across other websites.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. If you disable cookies entirely, some parts of this site may not work as expected, but you can still use every part of our service by messaging us on WhatsApp.

10. Children's data

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

We do alter school uniforms and children's garments, but these are always provided to us by a parent or guardian — the contract is with the adult, not the child. Where we do hold limited information about a child (for example, garment size to alter a school uniform correctly), it is provided by the parent or guardian, kept only as long as needed to complete the job, and deleted on request.

If you believe we have collected personal data from a child under 18 without a parent's or guardian's consent, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

We are a Kenyan business and we store customer data primarily in Kenya. Some of the services we rely on — including WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) and our website host — may process data in other countries.

Where a transfer of your data outside Kenya occurs, we rely on service providers who offer appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual protections and recognised data protection frameworks. If you would like more information about a specific transfer, contact us using the details in Section 15.
