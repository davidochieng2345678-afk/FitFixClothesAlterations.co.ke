1. Who we are

We provide alterations, repairs and bespoke tailoring services with free door-to-door pickup and delivery across Nairobi.

By using our services — by sending us a garment for alteration, by booking a pickup, or by instructing us to carry out any work — you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with any part of them, please do not use our services.

Our business address is Industrial Area, Nairobi 00500, Kenya. Our contact details are in Section 15.

2. Services we provide

We provide alteration, repair and bespoke tailoring services. These typically fall into the following categories:

  • Top-half alterations — shirts, t-shirts, polo shirts, sweaters and knitwear. Sleeve shortening, body slimming, collar and cuff work, hem repairs.
  • Bottom-half alterations — trousers, chinos, jeans, shorts, cargo pants, track pants and dungarees. Hemming, waist take-in and let-out, seat and rise adjustment, leg slimming, zip and elastic replacement.
  • Outerwear alterations — suits, blazers, sports coats, casual jackets, trenchcoats, overcoats and leather jackets. Sleeve work, body slimming, shoulder adjustment, jacket lengthening and shortening, lapel roll correction, lining work.
  • Formal and special wear — waistcoats, bridal and formal wear, school uniforms, kitenge and African wear.
  • Repairs — zipper replacement, fabric patching, darning and invisible mending, elastic replacement, strap adjustment, re-lining, button and snap replacement.
  • Bespoke tailoring — custom-made garments drafted to your measurements from fabric you supply or that we source on your behalf.

Our services are provided by David Ochieng, Master Tailor and founder, personally. Where additional help is used (for example, a rider for pickup and delivery), it is under his direct supervision and to the same standard.

3. Quotes and payment

How we quote

Every job starts with a fixed price sent to you on WhatsApp. Quotes are based on the photo and description you send us. If the garment turns out to be more complex when we receive it in the workshop, we message you before proceeding — never after — and confirm the revised price with you.

The quote we send is the price you pay, provided the garment matches the description and photos you gave us. Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date we send them.

What's included in the quote

Every quote includes:

  • Free pickup of the garment from your home or office.
  • Free delivery of the completed work back to your door.
  • Any adjustments under our fit guarantee (see Section 8).

Payment

  • Payment is due on delivery — after you've received the finished garment and are satisfied with the fit.
  • We accept M-Pesa, cash, and bank transfer.
  • For bespoke tailoring and large batches (typically 10 or more garments), a 50% deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due on delivery.
  • We do not store card details — all payments are made via M-Pesa, cash or direct bank transfer, and confirmations are handled manually.

4. Turnaround times

We give every client an honest turnaround estimate in the initial WhatsApp quote. The typical times are:

  • Standard alterations — 1–3 working days. Examples: hemming, zip replacement, elastic replacement, simple repairs, button replacement.
  • Complex alterations — 3–5 working days. Examples: waist take-in, leg slimming, sleeve shortening, hem work on multiple garments, suit sleeve work.
  • Advanced alterations — 5–7 working days. Examples: shoulder adjustment, jacket lengthening, lining replacement, chino and trouser rebuilding.
  • Specialist work — 6–8 working days. Examples: full restyles, hand-finished work on bespoke garments, quilting, hand-darning on luxury fibres.
  • Bespoke tailoring — 3 to 4 weeks. This includes two fittings, a final fitting and any minor adjustments agreed during those fittings.
  • Rush service — where the deadline can be met, an additional KSh 500 is charged. Rush service is subject to availability and can't be applied to work that physically requires more time (for example, blocking time on knitwear, or hand-finishing on a canvas blazer).

Turnaround times are estimates given in good faith. Where a delay is caused by something outside our control — a rider breakdown, a public holiday, a nationwide shortage of a specific zip, or a seasonal surge during January or September term opening — we tell you as soon as we know, not after the deadline has passed.

5. Your responsibilities

To get the best result and to keep the alteration work straightforward, we ask you to:

  • Send accurate photos and descriptions. The photos you send us should show the whole garment plus close-ups of any problem area. If the garment turns out to be materially different from what the photos showed, we may need to re-quote — and we'll always ask before proceeding.
  • Tell us about prior alterations. If the garment has already been altered by someone else, tell us at the quote stage. A second alteration on a previously-altered garment is sometimes not possible and sometimes carries extra risk — we'd rather know before we start.
  • Bring the garment in a clean state. We can work on garments that have been worn, but heavily soiled garments (food, oil, paint) can affect the fabric during alteration. If your garment is heavily soiled, cleaning it before pickup is best for both of us.
  • Attend fittings for bespoke work. Bespoke tailoring includes two fittings. If a fitting is missed, we adjust the schedule but the completion date shifts accordingly.
  • Collect the finished garment. We hold completed garments for up to 30 days after we notify you of completion, after which storage fees may apply (see Section 10).

6. Our responsibilities

In return, we commit to:

  • Quoting honestly and in writing. The price we send is the price you pay. Where a job turns out to be more complex, we ask before proceeding.
  • Treating every garment as if it were ours. Delicate fibres, luxury garments and heirloom pieces are treated with the appropriate care. On any garment we can't alter cleanly, we say so before taking the job.
  • Using appropriate techniques for the garment. Woven cloth is altered like woven cloth; knitwear is altered like knitwear; leather is handled like leather. We don't substitute a faster technique where a slower one is required.
  • Delivering in the timeframe agreed. Where a delay is unavoidable, we tell you immediately and offer either a revised date or a full refund of any deposit.
  • Communicating clearly. Every message we send is answerable within working hours. You can reach David directly throughout the process.
  • Honouring the fit guarantee. Every alteration is covered by our fit guarantee (see Section 8).

7. Liability for garments

We take the following approach to liability. This section is written plainly, because we'd rather you understood it than glossed over it.

What we're responsible for

  • Any damage we cause during alteration. If we tear, scorch, mark or otherwise damage your garment during work, we'll repair it at our cost. If it can't be repaired, we'll compensate you fairly — see the limits below.
  • Any garment lost while in our care. If a garment is lost between collection and delivery, we'll reimburse you fairly.

What we're not responsible for

  • Pre-existing weakness in the fabric. Garments whose fabric has thinned, dried out or become brittle are prone to failing during alteration — that's why we check and flag any weakness we see before starting. If you've told us to proceed anyway, the risk of that specific failure is yours.
  • Normal wear, fading or shrinkage that happens after collection.
  • Damage from washing, drying or cleaning after you've received the garment back.
  • Damage from moths or other pests while the garment is stored with you.
  • Any loss of sentimental value where the fabric itself is intact.

Maximum liability

Where we are found to be responsible for loss or damage to a garment, our maximum liability is the greater of:

  • The cost of the alteration service you paid for; or
  • The current market value of a comparable garment (based on the receipt you provide, or where no receipt exists, the estimated replacement cost of the same garment or the closest available equivalent).

We do not accept liability for any indirect losses — for example, a missed event, a missed flight, or the cost of hiring a replacement garment for a specific occasion. If your garment is needed by a hard deadline, tell us at the quote stage and we'll confirm before starting whether we can meet it.

8. Fit guarantee and remedies

Every alteration we carry out is covered by our fit guarantee. This is the primary way we resolve any concern and the one we're most proud of keeping.

What the fit guarantee covers

  • The finished alteration doesn't match the measurement we agreed with you.
  • The garment doesn't sit correctly on your body — the hem hangs unevenly, the sleeve twists, the waist gapes when you sit, the collar gapes at the neck.
  • A seam or finish we added has failed — a stitch has come loose, a hem has opened, a zip we fitted has stopped working.
  • A visible mark, needle hole or press mark we made during the alteration that wasn't there before.
  • You've changed your mind about the exact length or width after seeing the finished result.

What the fit guarantee means in practice

We collect the garment again and adjust the work free of charge. There's no time limit on a fit adjustment — if you wore the garment for six months and only now realise the sleeve is slightly short, message us and we'll fix it. In fourteen years of running this workshop, we have never charged a client for a re-do.

Where adjustments are physically limited

Some alterations have hard physical limits — a shoulder seam can only be reduced so far, a hem can only be let down as far as the allowance allows, a lengthened jacket can only be lengthened as much as the cloth allows. Where an adjustment is limited in this way, we explained it before starting the job. In those specific cases, the fit guarantee remains in force but the adjustment is capped at the physical limit of the garment.

9. Cancellation and refunds

Before work begins

You may cancel your order before we've started cutting, stitching, sourcing fabric or opening any seam — free of charge. Message us on WhatsApp and the job is cancelled. We return the garment on the same rider that collected it.

After work has begun

If you cancel after we've started the work — cutting fabric, opening seams, sourcing a matching lining, or hand-finishing a panel — a cancellation fee of 10% of the quoted price applies. This covers the materials we've committed and the labour we can't recover. The remaining 90% of the quote is refunded to you.

If we cancel the job

If we ever cancel or turn down a job after accepting it — which we only do if we discover something we couldn't see in the original photos, such as moth damage throughout a coat — you get a full refund of any payment and the garment is returned to you at our cost.

Refunds

Refunds are the exception, not the rule, because the fit guarantee resolves almost everything. When we do issue a refund, it's because either we couldn't achieve the fit after a reasonable adjustment, or we damaged the garment during alteration. Refunds cover the alteration fee, not the cost of the garment itself. See our returns and refund policy for the full process.

10. Uncollected garments

Where a garment has been completed and we have notified you of completion on WhatsApp, we hold it for 30 days at no charge. After 30 days, a storage fee of KSh 200 per week, per garment, may apply. After 90 days from the notification date, the garment may be sold or disposed of, in accordance with the Disposal of Uncollected Goods Act (Cap 38, Laws of Kenya), to recover our costs. We will always contact you before taking any disposal action.

11. Your rights under Kenyan law

These terms sit alongside, and do not replace, your statutory rights under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, 2012. Under that Act, you have the right to:

  • Services performed with reasonable care and skill. An alteration that leaves a garment in worse condition than it was, or that falls below the professional standard you paid for, is a breach of that right.
  • Services completed in a reasonable time where no specific time was agreed, and where the delay causes you genuine loss.
  • Clear and accurate information about the service, the price, and any material risks to the garment.
  • Redress where a service is not performed as agreed — including repair, repeat performance, or refund at your election, where those remedies are reasonable in the circumstances.
  • Protection against unfair terms in a consumer contract, and against conduct that is misleading or deceptive.
  • Access to the Competition Authority of Kenya (CAK) and the Consumer Protection Committee for the resolution of disputes where direct resolution has failed.

Nothing in these terms limits those rights. If a term of these terms would narrow what the Consumer Protection Act gives you, the Act prevails. Where the Act provides you a stronger remedy than our fit guarantee does, we'll honour the Act rather than hide behind our own terms.

Serious disputes may be referred by the CAK to the Consumer Protection Committee, which has power to order remedies including refunds and compensation.

Nothing in this clause removes your right to bring a claim in the Kenyan courts where that is what you choose to do.
