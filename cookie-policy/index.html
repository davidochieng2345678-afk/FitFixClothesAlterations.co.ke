Cookie Policy — FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke

FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke uses a small number of cookies to keep this website working properly and to understand which pages Nairobi visitors find most useful. This page explains exactly which cookies we set, why we set them, and how you can turn them off.

No legal jargon, no trickery, no selling your data. Just a straight explanation — the same way we'd explain a hem alteration on WhatsApp.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. The short version, before the legal bits

Most cookie policies are written by lawyers, for lawyers. This one isn't. Here's the honest summary before we go into detail.

If you only read one thing on this page, read this

We use cookies to keep this website working (essential cookies), to see which pages people visit (performance cookies from Google Analytics), and to remember your cookie choice so we don't ask again (a preference cookie). We do not sell your data. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles. We do not track you across other websites. You can turn all cookies off in your browser at any time — the website will still work, you'll just have a slightly less tailored experience.

That's the whole policy in one paragraph. Everything below is the detailed version, written out in full so the site complies with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and — for the sake of visitors from outside Kenya — with the principles of the EU's GDPR and similar frameworks.

2. What actually is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file — usually less than a kilobyte — that a website asks your browser to store on your device. That's it. It's not a program. It can't run code. It can't access other files on your phone or laptop. It can't see your photos, your messages, or your bank details.

What a cookie can do is remember a small piece of information and hand it back to the website the next time you visit. That's why you don't have to log in every time you open Gmail, and why an online shop remembers what's in your basket between visits.

On a tailoring website like ours, cookies do much less than on a shopping site — we don't have a customer login, we don't have a shopping cart, and we don't process payments on the site (you pay via M-Pesa on delivery, not online). Our cookies mostly do three things: keep the site running, tell us which service pages get read most often, and remember if you've already dismissed our cookie notice.

There is one small technicality worth knowing. A cookie is stored on your device, not on our servers. We can't go looking through your phone for it. The only way we ever "see" what's in a cookie is if your browser sends it back to us on your next visit — and only for the same domain that set it in the first place.

Cookies vs other similar technologies

Browsers also use things called local storage and session storage — which work like cookies but hold more data and don't get sent back to the server on every request. In this policy, we use the word "cookie" as shorthand for all of these tracking technologies, because from your point of view as a visitor, they do the same job.

3. How FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke uses cookies

We use cookies for four specific purposes. Here's what each one is, in plain language, and what happens if you turn it off.

3.1 Essential cookies — the site won't work without them

Essential cookies are what let a website function at all. On FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke, they do things like:

  • Keep your session open while you click through service pages.
  • Protect the contact form from spam bots (we use a lightweight anti-spam check).
  • Remember your cookie consent choice so you aren't asked again on every page.
  • Ensure the WhatsApp widget and contact buttons load correctly.

These cookies don't collect any personal information. They don't know your name, your email, or where you're from. They can't be switched off individually — without them, the site would break. You can still block them in your browser, but some features may stop working.

3.2 Performance cookies — how we know which pages people actually read

Performance cookies tell us things like: which service pages are visited most often, how long people spend on each page, which buttons they click, and where they land from (Google search, a WhatsApp link, a direct visit). We use Google Analytics 4 for this — it's the industry standard, used by millions of websites.

All data from Google Analytics is aggregated and anonymised by the time we see it. We don't get a report saying "David from Runda read the cargo pant page at 3:47pm." We get a report saying "the cargo pant page had 340 visits this month, average time on page 2 minutes 15 seconds, mostly from mobile devices in Nairobi." That's the level of detail we work with.

Performance cookies help us decide which services to write more detail about, which pages to improve, and whether our content is reaching the right people in Nairobi. If you turn them off, the site still works exactly the same for you — we just lose the anonymous data point.

3.3 Functionality cookies — remembering your preferences

Functionality cookies remember choices you make on the site so you don't have to make them again. On our website, that's mostly:

  • Remembering whether you've already accepted or declined cookies.
  • Remembering your preferred language if we ever add a Kiswahili version of the site.
  • Remembering the last service page you viewed, in case you want to return to it.

These are lightweight and short-lived. They're about convenience, not tracking. If you turn them off, you'll just be asked the same questions again on each visit.

3.4 What we do NOT do with cookies

To be clear and unambiguous — because we know this matters:

  • We do not sell cookie data to third parties.
  • We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles or retarget you with ads on Facebook or Google.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not use cookies to collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not run behavioural advertising, pixel tracking, or any form of cross-site profiling.
  • We do not ask you to accept cookies before you can use the site — you can read every page without ever clicking "accept."

In short: our cookie use is minimal, boring, and functional. That's exactly how we want it to be.

4. The cookies we actually set — by category

Here's a breakdown of the cookie categories we use, in visual form. Every cookie on FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke falls into one of these three groups.

Essential

Session & security cookies

These keep the site running and protect the contact form from abuse. They're set the moment you arrive and cleared when you close your browser (session cookies) or after a short period (persistent cookies for spam protection).

ExamplesPHPSESSID, csrf_token, cookie_consent — technical names you may see if you inspect your browser's cookie storage.

Performance

Analytics cookies

These tell us which pages people visit, how long they stay, and where they came from. All data is aggregated — we never see individual users. Google Analytics 4 is the service we use.

Examples_ga, _ga_<container-id>, _gid — standard Google Analytics cookie names you'll find on almost every business website in the world.

Functional

Preference cookies

These remember small choices you've made — like the fact that you've already dismissed our cookie banner, so we don't keep showing it every time you click to a new page.

Examplescookie_consent, last_service_viewed — mostly first-party cookies set by FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke itself.
