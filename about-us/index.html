Our Story — From Italian Menswear to Nairobi's Most Trusted Mobile Tailor

The story behind every stitch begins with fourteen years of professional tailoring experience. Here's how we got here.

2012 – 2018

Master Tailor at Manix Clothing Store — Italian Men's Wear

Where it all started

David Ochieng began his professional career at Manix Clothing Store, a premium Italian menswear boutique. Over six years he learned the foundations of Italian tailoring — clean lines, structured shoulders and the art of balanced proportions — working alongside experienced Italian-trained tailors on premium suits and jackets.

2018 – 2022

Senior Tailor at Italian Men's Wear

Mastering complex alterations

At Italian Men's Wear, David stepped into a senior role, taking charge of complex alterations and bespoke fittings. This is where he mastered suit shoulder reduction, jacket lengthening and full suit restructuring — the three most difficult alterations in menswear, and the three most tailors refuse to attempt.

2022 – 2024

Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd

Managing a team, serving premium clients

As Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd, David managed a team of tailors and oversaw hundreds of alterations per month. He introduced quality-control systems, streamlined the alteration workflow and continued to personally handle high-value jobs for premium clients — a habit he still keeps today.

2024 – Present

Founder of FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke

Bringing Italian expertise to every Nairobi door

In 2024 David fulfilled a long-held dream by founding FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke. His vision was simple: offer every Nairobi resident access to world-class tailoring with the convenience of free door-to-door pickup and delivery across every estate. Today he personally oversees every alteration that leaves our Industrial Area workshop — which is exactly why the fit is consistently right.

Every alteration is measured, checked and re-checked. We do not cut corners — because a millimetre off is a millimetre visible on your garment. Fourteen years of experience means we know exactly how much fabric to take in, how much to let out, and when to leave something alone.
