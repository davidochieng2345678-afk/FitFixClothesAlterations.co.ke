Returns & Refund Policy

"If the fit isn't right, we fix it. Free. That's the promise we've kept for fourteen years — and it's the reason this page mostly explains what we don't refund, because the fit guarantee makes refunds rare."

This policy explains our fit guarantee, when refunds apply, cancellation terms, and your rights as a consumer under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, 2012.

📅 Effective from 15 January 2026 🔄 Last updated 24 September 2026

The short version

  • Fit guarantee: if the alteration isn't right, we collect the garment again and adjust it free. No time limit, no argument. This is our primary remedy and it's what most concerns get resolved by.
  • Refunds apply only where we cannot complete the work to a reasonable standard, or where we damage a garment during alteration. Refunds cover the alteration fee, not the cost of the garment itself.
  • Cancellation: free before work begins. 10% of the quoted price applies once materials or labour have been committed. Bespoke tailoring deposits are non-refundable once work has started.
  • Request window: contact us within 7 days of collecting your garment for any refund or adjustment request. Fit guarantee adjustments are welcome at any time.
  • We don't refund garments that were already damaged, worn, washed after collection, or where the fabric was weak before alteration.
  • Your consumer rights under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, 2012 are unaffected by this policy and apply in addition to it.

1. The FitFix fit guarantee

Before we talk about refunds, we want to be clear about the primary way we resolve problems — because it's how ninety-nine out of a hundred concerns are handled at FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke.

If an alteration doesn't fit the way it should, or the finished garment isn't what you asked for, we collect it again and adjust it free of charge. We don't charge for the second trip. We don't charge for the second alteration. We don't apply a time limit on fit adjustments — if you wore the garment for six months and only now realise the sleeve is sitting slightly short, message us and we'll fix it.

This is the guarantee we've kept for fourteen years of running the workshop. In that time, we have never charged a client for a re-do. It's the reason refunds are rare on our side of the counter — the fit guarantee resolves most issues before refunds come into the conversation at all.

What counts as "not right"

The fit guarantee covers any of the following:

  • The finished alteration doesn't match the measurement we agreed with you.
  • The garment doesn't sit correctly on your body — the hem hangs unevenly, the sleeve twists, the waist gapes when you sit.
  • A seam or finish we added has failed (a stitch has come loose, a hem has opened, a zip we fitted has stopped working).
  • A visible mark, needle hole or press mark we made during the alteration that wasn't on the garment before.
  • You've changed your mind about the exact length or width after seeing the finished result.

In each of those cases, the fix is a free adjustment — not a refund. We complete the adjustment in the same standard turnaround as any other job, and we deliver it back to you at no cost.

2. What's covered and what isn't

To make this as clear as possible, here's a side-by-side breakdown of what we cover and what we don't. If your situation isn't listed here, message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you honestly which side of the line it falls on.

✓ We cover

  • Free adjustment of any fit issue, at any time, under the fit guarantee.
  • Free repair of any stitch, seam or finish we made that has failed.
  • Free repair of any damage we caused to your garment during alteration.
  • Refund of the alteration fee if we can't get the fit right after a reasonable adjustment.
  • Refund of the alteration fee if we damage a garment beyond reasonable repair.
  • Cancellation before work begins, at no cost.
  • Full refund of a deposit if we cancel the job for any reason.

✕ We don't cover

  • The cost of the garment itself — we only handle the alteration fee.
  • Pre-existing damage, wear or weakness in the fabric.
  • Garments that were worn or washed after collection and have developed new damage.
  • Changes in your body size or shape after we completed the alteration.
  • Bespoke garments once they've been cut to your measurements (see Section 5).
  • Damage from laundering or dry cleaning after collection.
  • Damage the original maker left in the garment before we received it.

3. Refunds — eligibility and process

Refunds are the exception, not the rule, because the fit guarantee resolves almost everything. When we do issue a refund, it's because one of two things happened: either we couldn't achieve the fit after a reasonable adjustment, or we damaged the garment during alteration beyond what we could reasonably repair.

What a refund covers

  • The alteration fee you paid for the job — full or partial depending on what went wrong.
  • Any extra charge you paid for a specific service (a rush fee, a fabric upgrade, a specialist repair).

What a refund does not cover

  • The cost of the garment you brought to us — that's between you and whoever sold it to you.
  • Any loss, loss of use, or consequential cost associated with the alteration.
  • The cost of a replacement alteration done elsewhere.

How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued via the same payment method you used for the original job:

  • M-Pesa: refunded to the same M-Pesa number the payment came from. Processed within 3 working days of approval.
  • Cash: refunded in cash at the workshop, or sent via M-Pesa if more convenient. Processed within 3 working days of approval.
  • Bank transfer: refunded to the same bank account. Processed within 7 working days of approval to allow for inter-bank settlement.

4. Cancellation policy

We understand that plans change, and we try to be fair to both sides on cancellations.

Before work begins — no charge

If you cancel your order before we have started cutting, stitching, sourcing fabric or opening a seam on your garment, there is no charge. Message us on WhatsApp and the job is cancelled free of charge. We'll return the garment on the same rider that collected it.

Once work has begun — 10% of the quoted price

If you cancel after we've started the work — cutting fabric, opening seams, sourcing a matching lining, or hand-finishing a panel — a cancellation fee of 10% of the quoted price applies. This covers the materials we've committed and the labour we can't recover. The remaining 90% of the quote is refunded to you.

If we cancel the job — full refund

If we ever cancel or turn down a job after accepting it (which we only do if we discover something we couldn't see in the original photos, such as moth damage throughout a coat), you get a full refund of any payment and the garment is returned to you at our cost.

Why a 10% fee on late cancellation? Because by the time you cancel, we've usually already cut fabric to your measurements or ordered a specific lining or zip that can't be returned. The 10% covers the material cost and the bench time, and it's cheaper than the industry standard — some tailors charge up to 30% for a cancellation after work has begun.

5. Bespoke and bridal commissions

Bespoke tailoring, bridal work and any commission where we're drafting a garment from scratch for you are handled slightly differently, because the garment is being made specifically for you and cannot be resold.

Deposits

Bespoke and bridal commissions require a deposit to begin, usually 50% of the quoted price. Once we've started cutting or drafting a pattern for your measurements, the deposit is non-refundable — the fabric has been cut for you and cannot be repurposed.

Fittings

We schedule two fittings minimum on every bespoke commission, and a final fitting before collection. Any adjustments from those fittings are included in the original price — we don't charge for the natural back-and-forth of getting a bespoke fit right. If after the final fitting the fit is still wrong through our own error, we correct it free under the fit guarantee, or refund the deposit if we genuinely cannot get it right.

Bridal timelines

Bridal work is scheduled around your wedding date, and we plan the schedule conservatively. If you change your wedding date, tell us as early as possible — we'll adjust the schedule where we can. If a date change means we can't complete the work in time, we'll tell you honestly and either refund the deposit or agree an alternative plan with you.

  • Acknowledgement: within 48 hours of your message, usually much sooner during working hours.
  • Collection of the garment (if needed): within 24 hours.
  • Assessment: 1–2 working days.
  • Adjustment (fit guarantee): same turnaround as any standard job — 2 to 5 working days depending on the work.

  • Refund paid out: 3 working days for M-Pesa and cash; 7 working days for bank transfer.

If at any point in the process you haven't heard from us within the times above, message again — sometimes WhatsApp hides a message, and we'd rather you chased us than stayed silent.

8. Exceptions

There are a few situations where a refund or free adjustment doesn't apply. We list them here so there are no surprises.

  • Post-collection damage: garment damaged after you took it home, whether by wear, laundering, ironing, moths in storage or an accident.
  • Washing-related failure: linings, elastics and stitching that fail because a garment was machine-washed or tumble-dried against the care advice we give on collection.
  • Body size changes: if your body size or shape has changed since we completed the alteration, a fit guarantee adjustment is available but may be quoted as a fresh job rather than a free re-do — because the garment now needs to be re-fitted to a different body, not corrected.
  • Pre-existing garment weakness: where fabric was already worn, thinned or weak before we received it, and the alteration has revealed that weakness. We flag any weakness we see before starting work, and we ask your permission to proceed in writing (via WhatsApp) when we spot it.
  • Garments supplied with incorrect measurements: if you gave us measurements we later find to be wrong (for example a garment you sent by rider without trying it on), we adjust free under the fit guarantee once — subsequent corrections are quoted normally.
  • Bespoke commissions after cutting: see Section 5.

9. Your rights as a consumer in Kenya

This policy sits alongside, and does not replace, your statutory rights under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, 2012. Under that Act, you have the right to:

  • Services performed with reasonable care and skill. An alteration that leaves a garment in worse condition than it was, or that clearly falls below the professional standard you paid for, is a breach of that right.
  • Services completed in a reasonable time where no specific time was agreed, and where the delay causes you genuine loss.
  • Clear and accurate information about the service, the price, and any material risks to the garment.
  • Redress where a service is not performed as agreed — including repair, repeat performance, or refund at your election, where those remedies are reasonable in the circumstances.
  • Protection against unfair terms in a consumer contract, and against conduct that is misleading or deceptive.
  • Access to the Consumer Protection Committee (and the Competition Authority of Kenya) for the resolution of disputes where direct resolution has failed.

Nothing in this policy limits those rights. If a term of this policy would narrow what the Consumer Protection Act gives you, the Act prevails. Where the Act provides you a stronger remedy than our fit guarantee does, we'll honour the Act rather than hide behind our own paperwork. Our guarantee is here to add to your rights, not to replace them.

10. How to complain

If you're unhappy with how we've handled an alteration, a refund request, or an adjustment, please raise it with us first — we would much rather fix it directly and quickly than have it go anywhere else. In fourteen years, we've resolved every complaint that has come to us directly.

Step 1 — Contact us directly. WhatsApp David on +254 705 728 896, or email hello@fitfixclothesalterations.co.ke. Tell us what happened and what outcome you want. We will acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 14 days.

Step 2 — Escalate to the Competition Authority of Kenya (CAK). If you are not satisfied with our response, or if you believe we have acted contrary to the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Competition Authority of Kenya, which administers consumer protection in Kenya.

CAK contact: Competition Authority of Kenya, Nairobi. Website: cak.go.ke. Email: info@cak.go.ke.

Step 3 — Consumer Protection Committee. Serious disputes may be referred by the CAK to the Consumer Protection Committee, which has power to order remedies including refunds and compensation.
