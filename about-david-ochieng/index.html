A Fourteen-Year Journey in Italian Menswear Tailoring

David Ochieng's career has been shaped by three of Nairobi's most respected menswear houses. Each role added a new layer of expertise — and each one now shows in every garment he touches.

2012 – 2018

Master Tailor — Manix Clothing Store, Italian Men's Wear

Where the craft was built

David began his professional tailoring career at Manix Clothing Store, a premium Italian menswear boutique in Nairobi. Over six years he learned the foundations of Italian tailoring — clean lines, structured shoulders, and the art of balanced proportions. Working alongside experienced Italian-trained tailors, he developed a deep understanding of how a well-fitted suit should move with the wearer. His work at Manix focused primarily on premium suit alterations, trouser hemming and jacket adjustments for Nairobi's corporate elite and retail clientele.

2018 – 2022

Senior Tailor — Italian Men's Wear

Mastering the hardest alterations in menswear

At Italian Men's Wear, David stepped into a senior role, taking charge of complex alterations and bespoke fittings. This is where he mastered suit shoulder reduction, jacket lengthening and full suit restructuring — three of the most difficult alterations in menswear, and the three most tailors refuse to attempt. He also became the go-to tailor for wedding suits and formal wear, ensuring grooms and their parties looked impeccable on the day. During this period David refined his expertise in delicate Italian fabrics — wool, cashmere, linen and premium blends.

2022 – 2024

Head Tailor — City Walk Ltd

Leading a team, serving premium Nairobi clients

As Head Tailor at City Walk Ltd, David managed a team of tailors and oversaw hundreds of alterations per month. He introduced quality-control systems, streamlined the alteration workflow, and continued to personally handle high-value jobs for premium clients — a habit he still keeps today. His work at City Walk solidified his reputation as a leading Italian-trained custom suit tailor in Nairobi, and it is where he developed the strict quality standard that FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke is built on.

2024 – Present

Founder & Master Tailor — FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke

Bringing world-class tailoring to every Nairobi door

In 2024, David fulfilled a long-held dream by founding FitFixClothesAlterations.co.ke, headquartered in Industrial Area, Nairobi. His vision was simple: to offer every Nairobi resident access to world-class tailoring with the convenience of free door-to-door pickup and delivery across every estate in the city. Today he personally oversees every alteration that leaves the workshop — which is exactly why the fit is consistently right. Whether you need to order trusted bespoke tailoring in Nairobi from an experienced master tailor or simply need a favourite jacket rescued, David handles it himself.
