Why Should You Care About a Business Registration Number?

You might be wondering, "Why does it matter if my tailor is registered with the government? I just want my trousers hemmed." While it is true that anyone with a sewing machine can alter a pair of jeans, the stakes change dramatically when you are dealing with high-value garments, bespoke tailoring, and luxury fabrics.

Here is a deep dive into exactly why our legal registration status should give you absolute peace of mind:

1. Legal Recourse and Consumer Protection

If you hire an unregistered, informal tailor and they ruin your Ksh 50,000 suit, you have very little legal recourse. You cannot easily sue an individual with no registered business address or legal entity. However, because FitFix is a registered business, you are protected under the Kenyan Consumer Protection Act. If we fail to deliver on our promises, you have the full weight of the law behind you to seek compensation. We are accountable.

2. Protection of Your Garments

When you use our free pickup and delivery service, a rider comes to your home or office to collect your garments. This requires immense trust. Because we are a registered business with a verified physical location and verified identity, you know exactly who has your clothes. If a garment is lost or damaged in transit, our business insurance and legal standing ensure that you are compensated. An informal tailor operating from a roadside stall does not offer this protection.

3. Financial Accountability and Anti-Money Laundering

Registered businesses are required to maintain transparent financial records, use traceable payment methods, and comply with Kenyan tax laws. When you pay FitFix, you receive a proper invoice and receipt. You are not handing cash to an anonymous individual in a back alley. This financial accountability protects both you and us from fraud and ensures the business operates on sustainable, ethical principles.

4. Quality Assurance and Longevity

A business that has invested time, money, and effort into registering, branding, and legally establishing itself is highly unlikely to disappear overnight. We have built our reputation over years of hard work. We have a physical workshop, a team of verified employees, and a long-term vision. This means when you invest in our bespoke tailoring services, you can trust that we will be there next month, next year, and in five years to honor our workmanship warranty.

5. Compliance with Health and Safety Standards

A registered business is subject to inspections and must comply with local health, safety, and environmental regulations. Our workshop is a clean, safe, and well-lit environment. Your garments are not being altered in a dusty, moldy, unregulated backroom. We invest in proper lighting, industrial sewing machines, and safe storage facilities to ensure your clothes are treated with the utmost care and hygiene.

The FitFix Story: How We Built a Tailoring Empire on Trust

FitFix was founded by David Ochieng' Kiruo, a master tailor with a vision to elevate the standard of tailoring in Nairobi. David recognized that the industry was plagued by inconsistent quality, poor customer service, and a lack of accountability. He saw men and women settling for ill-fitting clothes because they didn't trust anyone to alter them properly.

David started with a simple philosophy: treat every garment as if it were your own, and treat every client like a VIP. Over the years, that philosophy has evolved into a comprehensive tailoring business that offers:

  • Bespoke Made-to-Measure: Creating custom garments from scratch, including suits, shirts, and trousers, tailored perfectly to the client's body.
  • High-End Alterations: Rescuing ill-fitting suits, resizing wedding dresses, and perfecting the fit of everyday wear.

  • Logistical Convenience: We pioneered the free door-to-door pickup and delivery model in Nairobi, making professional tailoring accessible to busy professionals.

Today, FitFix is recognized as one of the most trusted tailoring brands in Nairobi. Our clients include corporate executives, diplomats, grooms, and fashion-conscious individuals who demand the very best. The business registration certificate is just one piece of paper, but it represents the culmination of years of hard work, ethical business practices, and an unwavering commitment to our clients.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Registration

How can I verify your business registration?

You can verify our business name and registration number (BN-B8S6O3PE) directly through the Kenyan Business Registration Service (BRS) eCitizen portal. You can also visit our workshop in the Industrial Area to physically view the original certificate.

Are you insured for loss or damage?

Yes. As a registered business, we carry liability insurance. While we take every precaution to protect your garments, in the highly unlikely event of a loss or irreparable damage caused by our negligence, our insurance policy covers the cost of compensating you for the value of the garment.

Do you offer official receipts and invoices?

Absolutely. For every transaction, whether you pay via M-Pesa or bank transfer, we issue an official, stamped receipt. For our corporate clients, we provide fully compliant tax invoices.

Is my personal data safe with you?

Yes. We strictly adhere to the Kenya Data Protection Act. Your measurements, phone number, and delivery address are used solely for the purpose of fulfilling your order and are never sold or shared with third parties.

What if I am not satisfied with the work?

We encourage you to speak to us immediately. Because we are a registered business, we have a formal complaints procedure. We will do everything in our power to rectify the issue, whether that means re-doing the alteration, refunding your money, or compensating you for the garment. We value our reputation above all else.
