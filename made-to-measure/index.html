Why Off-the-Rack Clothing Never Fits Nairobi Professionals

Most working professionals in Nairobi buy clothing cut for a standard size chart that does not match their body. The result is a wardrobe that looks acceptable on a hanger and feels wrong by the end of the day. Made-to-measure Nairobi clothing solves that problem at the source.

  • Shirts that pull across the chest or gape at the collar by mid-morning.
  • Trousers that fit the waist but are too wide in the seat, or fit the seat but pinch at the waist.
  • Blazers that sit too long in the body, ride up at the shoulders, or bulge at the button line.
  • Dresses and skirts that feel fine standing still and wrong the moment you sit down in a meeting.

The problem is not your body. The problem is that factory sizing is built around an average that almost nobody matches. When your tailoring is built to your measurements, the fit stops being a compromise.

How Made-to-Measure Works

Five steps from the first WhatsApp message to a garment that fits properly. Every step is handled in Nairobi, with no need to visit a workshop unless you want to.

  1. Book a Consultation

    Send us a WhatsApp message and we schedule a visit to your home or office anywhere in Nairobi. No cost, no obligation.

  2. Pick Your Fabric

    We bring fabric samples for you to see and feel. Choose your fabric, your colour, and the construction details you want.

  3. Get Measured

    A full set of measurements is taken on site. Chest, waist, seat, thigh, inseam, sleeve, shoulder, and any specifics for the garment you are ordering.

  4. First Fitting

    Your garment is basted and fitted on you before final stitching. Small adjustments are made at this stage so the finished piece sits correctly.

  5. Final Delivery

    We deliver the finished garment to your address, pressed and ready to wear. Payment balance is settled on delivery by M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about made-to-measure clothing at FitFix in Nairobi.

How long does made-to-measure take?

Standard turnaround is 10 to 14 days from your measurement appointment, with one fitting included. Shirts and trousers are usually ready at the earlier end of that range. Blazers and shift dresses are at the later end because of the extra construction involved. Rush turnaround is available on some items and is confirmed at your consultation.

Do I need to come to your workshop?

No. For made-to-measure in Nairobi we come to you. A fitting specialist visits your home or office anywhere in Nairobi to take measurements, present the fabric options and agree the details. The first fitting and final delivery are also handled at your address. If you would prefer to visit our workshop in Industrial Area, you are welcome to do that by appointment.

What if the fit is not perfect?

One fitting is included in every made-to-measure order and it is where small adjustments are usually made. If the garment still does not fit correctly after the fitting, we take it back and correct it at no cost. The perfect fit guarantee applies to every made-to-measure item we make in Nairobi.

What fabrics do you offer?

We offer premium cottons, cotton blends, wools, wool blends, linens and performance fabrics suited to Nairobi's climate. Fabric selection happens at your consultation with samples you can see and feel. We source from trusted suppliers and can also work with a fabric you provide if you have one you prefer.

How much is the deposit?

Made-to-measure orders in Nairobi are placed with a 50 percent deposit at the consultation. The remaining 50 percent is paid on delivery. We accept M-Pesa, cash and bank transfer. There is no fee for the consultation and no charge if you decide not to proceed.

Do you make plus-size clothing?

Yes. Made-to-measure is the best option for plus-size clothing because every pattern is cut to your measurements rather than to a standard block. We fit every body type and every size. There is no surcharge for larger sizes.

Can I order multiple items at once?

Yes, and it is usually the best way to build a workwear wardrobe. Our Workweek Capsule packages combine 3 shirts, 2 trousers and 1 blazer at a bundled price. Ordering multiple items together means one consultation, one set of measurements and one delivery for the whole set.
