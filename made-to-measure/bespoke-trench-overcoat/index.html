Why Made-to-Measure is the Ultimate Investment

We live in an era of fast fashion, where garments are mass-produced in standard sizes and expected to fit the diverse spectrum of human bodies. But the truth is, an "average" body does not exist. Every man and woman has a unique shoulder slope, a distinct posture, and a specific hip-to-waist ratio. When you buy off-the-rack clothing, you are settling for a compromise.

At FitFix Clothes Alterations, we reject the concept of the standard size. We believe that clothing should be a reflection of the individual, not a factory template. Our made-to-measure collection is born from a simple philosophy: when a garment fits you perfectly, you feel more confident, you look more authoritative, and you carry yourself with an undeniable presence.

This comprehensive guide explores our premier made-to-measure collections. From the sharpest executive suits to the most luxurious outerwear, we detail the craftsmanship, the fabrics, and the bespoke journey that goes into every stitch. Whether you are dressing for the boardroom, a wedding, or a weekend retreat, we have a bespoke solution that will transform your wardrobe.

1. The Bespoke Shirt: The Foundation of Style

A suit is only as good as the shirt beneath it. Yet, most men neglect their shirts, buying cheap, ill-fitting options that bunch at the waist, restrict the neck, and lose their shape after a few washes. A made-to-measure shirt is the easiest way to instantly elevate your appearance, whether you are wearing a tie or going open-collar.

Our custom shirts are engineered from the collar down. We do not just measure your neck and chest; we measure your shoulder slope, your arm length, and your preferred cuff circumference. We ask you how you wear your watch, as this affects the fit of the left sleeve. We even account for your posture—whether you stand straight, slouch, or have a prominent chest.

The Anatomy of a Bespoke Shirt

The Collar: This is the frame for your face. A poorly fitted collar will gap when you move your head, or it will choke you when you button it. We offer a variety of collar styles, including the classic spread collar (perfect for ties), the button-down (more casual), and the cutaway collar (modern and sharp).

The Cuffs: We offer barrel cuffs for everyday wear, French cuffs for formal occasions, and convertible cuffs that allow you to wear cufflinks when you want to. We ensure the cuff sits perfectly at the base of your wrist, leaving just enough room to comfortably wear a watch.

The Fabric: We source premium cotton from the world's finest mills. Choose from breathable Poplin for the Nairobi heat, Oxford for a more textured, casual look, or Sea Island Cotton for the ultimate luxury experience. We also offer custom made-to-measure shirts that blend local Kenyan flair with modern tailoring.

2. Luxury Outerwear: Trench Coats & Overcoats

When the Nairobi chill sets in—whether it's the early morning drive to Westlands or a late evening dinner in Karen—your outerwear is the first and last thing people see. It is the ultimate statement piece, the armor of the modern gentleman. A cheap, ill-fitting coat will ruin even the most expensive suit underneath, but a bespoke coat elevates your entire presence.

We specialize in two distinct categories of outerwear: the weatherproof trench coat and the insulating wool overcoat. Both are engineered to accommodate the bulk of a suit jacket while maintaining a sharp, flattering silhouette.

The Bespoke Men's Trench Coat: The Ultimate Defense

Most modern trench coats are made from cheap polyester that traps heat and feels clammy. Our bespoke trench coats are crafted from heritage fabrics like Ventile cotton and high-twist gabardine. Ventile, originally developed for WWII fighter pilots, uses a tightly woven cotton that swells when wet, creating a completely waterproof barrier while remaining highly breathable. We feature functional storm flaps, solid brass hardware, and a full floating canvas chest piece for a flawless drape.

6. The FitFix Bespoke Process

We have removed the guesswork and the hassle from custom clothing. Our process is designed for the busy professional who values quality but does not have hours to spend in a fitting room. Here is exactly how we bring your garments to life:

Step 1: The Consultation & Measurement

Everything begins with a quick WhatsApp chat or a private consultation at our workshop. We discuss your lifestyle, your existing wardrobe, and your preferences. We then take over a dozen precise measurements—not just your chest and waist, but your shoulder slope, arm length, posture, and more. Thanks to our free pickup and delivery service, we can do this at your home or office anywhere in Nairobi.

Step 2: Fabric Selection

We bring a curated selection of premium fabric swatches to your fitting. You can feel the weight, texture, and drape of each fabric. You will choose your outer fabric, your lining, and your button details. This is where the garment starts to feel truly yours.

Step 3: The Basted Fitting

Before we cut into your final fabric, we create a mock-up garment (a "toile") or use a basted fitting. This allows us to fine-tune the fit on your actual body. We check the shoulder alignment, the waist suppression, and the overall silhouette. We make adjustments until it is absolute perfection.

Step 4: Final Craftsmanship and Delivery

Our master tailor, David Ochieng, and his team hand-cut your fabric and construct the garment. We conduct a final quality check, ensuring every seam, stitch, and button is perfect. We deliver the garment to your door, and if any final minor tweaks are needed, we handle them under our workmanship warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is made-to-measure clothing better than off-the-rack?

Off-the-rack clothing is cut for a hypothetical average body, which means it rarely fits anyone perfectly. Made-to-measure clothing is drafted to your specific measurements, accommodating your unique shoulder slope, posture, and proportions. The result is a garment that looks and feels significantly better.

How long does it take to create a bespoke garment at FitFix?

Standard turnaround for most garments (shirts, chinos, joggers) is 7 to 14 days. Executive suits and trench coats may take 3 to 4 weeks due to the intricate handwork and multiple fitting sessions required.

Do you offer free pickup and delivery in Nairobi?

Yes. We offer complimentary door-to-door pickup and delivery across most Nairobi estates, including Westlands, Kilimani, Karen, Lavington, and the CBD, making the bespoke process convenient for busy professionals.

What is the best fabric for a suit in Nairobi's climate?

For Nairobi's warm days, we recommend lightweight worsted wool (Super 120s or 130s) or breathable cotton blends. These fabrics offer structure and drape while remaining highly breathable and wrinkle-resistant.

Can I get a custom garment if I have a unique body type?

Absolutely. That is the exact reason to choose made-to-measure. Whether you have broad shoulders, a narrow waist, larger thighs, or a petite frame, we adjust the pattern specifically for your body type, ensuring the garment flatters your unique silhouette.
