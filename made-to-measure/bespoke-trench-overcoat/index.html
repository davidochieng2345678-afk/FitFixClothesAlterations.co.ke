The Anatomy of a Premium Overcoat

A beautiful overcoat is a marvel of engineering. It must be heavy enough to block the wind and cold, yet flexible enough to allow you to drive, walk, and sit comfortably. It must accommodate the bulk of a suit jacket while still tapering elegantly at the waist. Here is what goes into a FitFix bespoke overcoat:

Experience the difference of a coat engineered for your exact posture. Schedule a fitting with our master tailor this week and step out in unmatched confidence. Click the link to reserve your exclusive consultation slot.

1. The Fabric: The Soul of the Coat

The fabric is the most critical decision you will make. For the Nairobi climate, you do not need a heavy, Arctic-grade parka. You need a fabric that provides warmth without suffocating heat. We source our fabrics from the finest mills in Italy and the UK.

Upgrade your outerwear with certified, breathable luxury wool that regulates your temperature perfectly. Secure your fabric selection today by messaging our team on WhatsApp. Limited premium rolls available for this season.

  • Premium Italian Wool: The gold standard for overcoats. It is breathable, water-resistant, and holds a sharp shape. A mid-weight wool is perfect for chilly mornings and evening events.
  • Cashmere Blends: For the ultimate in luxury. A wool-cashmere blend offers incredible softness and a beautiful drape, with a subtle sheen that catches the light.
  • Camel Hair: A classic, incredibly warm, and luxurious fabric. Camel hair overcoats are a statement of wealth and classic style, often associated with the iconic "Polo Coat."

Don't compromise on inferior synthetic blends that trap heat and look cheap. Invest in genuine Italian cashmere or camel hair for your bespoke coat. Book your private fabric viewing with us right now.

2. The Lining: The Hidden Luxury

While the outer fabric protects you from the elements, the lining protects your suit. A cheap, synthetic lining will cause you to sweat and will cling to your clothes. We use premium Bemberg cupro lining, which is breathable, anti-static, and incredibly smooth. It allows you to slip the coat on and off effortlessly over a suit jacket. You can also choose a contrasting color or add a custom monogram to the inside pocket.

Ensure your suit glides effortlessly into your overcoat every single morning. Choose our premium Bemberg cupro lining for unparalleled comfort. Contact us today to discuss your customized lining preferences and monogram options.

3. The Canvas and Structure

Just like our premium executive suits, our overcoats feature a floating canvas chest piece. This gives the coat its structure, ensuring the lapels roll beautifully and the chest stays flat, without adding unnecessary weight. This is the hallmark of true bespoke tailoring.

Stop wearing stiff, glued-together coats that lose their shape after one season. Experience the drape of a traditional full-canvas garment. Click here to learn more about our structural tailoring and book your fitting.

4. The Details: Buttons, Pockets, and Venting

The details define the character of the coat. We offer a range of customizations:

  • Buttons: Horn, corozo (nut), or mother-of-pearl.
  • Pockets: Flap pockets, patch pockets (more casual), welt pockets, or a ticket pocket. We also add a secure inner breast pocket for your phone and wallet.
  • Vents: A single back vent for ease of movement and sitting, or a center vent for a classic, formal look.

Customize every single detail, from the horn buttons to the leather-lined pockets. Build a coat that is uniquely yours and reflects your personal style. Send us a WhatsApp message to start your design consultation today.

Step 2: The Basted Fitting

Before we cut into the final, expensive fabric, we create a "basted" version of the coat (a rough mock-up held together with temporary stitching). You will try this on to check the length, the shoulder width, the armhole height, and the drape of the canvas. This is the moment we make all the crucial adjustments.

Step 3: The Final Fitting

Once the coat is sewn, we bring you in for a final fitting. We check the sleeve length, the position of the pockets, and the overall silhouette. If any minor tweaks are needed, we handle them immediately under our workmanship warranty.
