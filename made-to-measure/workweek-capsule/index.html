Why a Bundled Capsule Beats Buying Pieces Individually

Most working professionals in Nairobi buy their wardrobe piece by piece. A shirt here, a pair of trousers there, a blazer when the last one wore out. The result is a set of garments that fit tolerably well individually but never quite fit together, and the wardrobe never quite works.

  • You end up with three shirts that all coordinate with the same trousers and nothing that coordinates with the others.
  • The blazer was bought at a different time and does not sit in the same colour or fabric family as the trousers.
  • Each piece was measured on a different day and the fit is subtly inconsistent across the wardrobe.
  • You never quite escape the sense that you are dressing from a random selection rather than from a real wardrobe.
  • Every separate order means another consultation, another measurement session, another delivery.

The Workweek Capsule fixes that. Three shirts, two trousers and one blazer are chosen together, cut from the same measurements, pressed and delivered on the same day, and coordinated in colour and fabric so the whole set works as a wardrobe rather than as a pile of individual garments.

How You Customise the Capsule

The Workweek Capsule is not a fixed set. Every piece inside it is made to your measurements and built around the details you choose. The capsule is a framework that lets you coordinate the whole set in one consultation rather than in six separate ones.

Shirts

You choose the fabric (poplin, oxford, twill, linen blend or performance), the colour (plain white, blue, light grey, subtle stripe or check), the collar shape (spread, semi-spread, cutaway, button-down), the cuff style (single button, double button, mitred, rounded, French), the pocket (with or without), the placket, the back yoke and any monogram. Within the capsule you can mix shirt types: for example two dress shirts and one smart casual shirt, or three shirts in different weights for different seasons.

Trousers

You choose the fabric (cotton twill, cotton stretch, wool, wool blend), the colour (navy, charcoal, black, stone, beige), the cut (slim, tapered, straight, relaxed, wide leg), the rise (low, mid, high), the break (no break, half break, full break), the front (flat, single pleat, double pleat), the waistband (belt loops, side adjusters), the pockets (slanted, on-seam, welt, patch, coin, ticket), and the hem (plain, cuffed). Within the capsule you can mix trouser types: for example one chino and one dress trouser, or two in the same weight in different colours.

Blazer

You choose the fabric (cotton, cotton linen, linen, wool, wool blend, mohair), the shoulder construction (soft, natural, structured), the lapel (notch, peak, shawl, in the width you prefer), the button front (one-button, two-button, three-button, double-breasted), the lining (full, half, unlined, in matched or contrast colour), the pockets (flap, patch, welt, jetted, ticket), the vents (single, double, none), the button finish (horn, plastic matched or contrast, metal, mother of pearl), and the sleeve cuffs (plain, working with functional buttonholes).

The capsule rule: Every piece is cut to your measurements and every piece is coordinated with the others at the same consultation. The colour palette, the fabric weight and the formality level are chosen so the six garments work as a set, not just as individual pieces.

  5. Final Delivery

    The finished capsule is delivered to your address, pressed and ready to wear as a set. Balance is settled on delivery by M-Pesa, cash or bank transfer.
