Why Off-the-Rack Shirts Never Fit Nairobi Professionals

Most working professionals in Nairobi buy shirts cut to a standard size chart that matches almost nobody. The result is a shirt that looks fine on a hanger and feels wrong by the time you reach your desk.

  • Shirts that fit the neck but balloon at the waist.
  • Shirts that fit the chest but pull tight at the collar by mid-morning.
  • Sleeves that are too long, too short, or that stop halfway between the wrist and the knuckle.
  • Shoulders that sit an inch too wide or an inch too narrow because the size chart splits the difference.
  • Shirt tails that come untucked the moment you sit down in a meeting.

The problem is not your body. Factory sizing is built around an average that almost nobody matches. When a shirt is cut to your measurements, the fit stops being a compromise and starts being correct.

Made-to-Measure Shirts for Men and Women in Nairobi

The construction logic is different for men's shirts and women's shirts, and the measurements are different. Both are handled on the same bench, with the same fabric range and the same fit guarantee.

Men's made-to-measure shirts

Men's shirts are cut to your neck, chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve length and sleeve width. The pattern is adjusted at each of those points so the shirt sits cleanly without pulling anywhere. We take specific measurements for the shoulder slope, the armhole, the cuff opening and the shirt length, because those are the points where an off-the-rack shirt fails most often for Nairobi men. The collar is set to your neck measurement plus a small allowance for comfort, so the shirt sits properly without being tight. Sleeve length is measured to the wrist bone, not to the base of the thumb, so the cuff sits at the correct point on the hand.

Men's made-to-measure shirts start from KSh 3,000. Details are confirmed at your consultation. The most common details chosen by Nairobi professionals are a semi-spread collar, single-button mitred cuffs, no pocket, a plain placket and a small monogram on the cuff or the tail. Every one of those is optional.

Women's made-to-measure shirts

Women's shirts are cut to a different base pattern that accounts for the bust, the waist and the hip, with shaping at the bust and the side seams. The measurements we take for women's shirts include the bust, the underbust, the natural waist, the hip, the shoulder, the sleeve length and the shirt length. Where a client wants a shirt that skims rather than shapes, we cut it with a straighter silhouette. Where a client wants a shaped shirt, we cut it with bust darts and side shaping. Both are made from the same fabric range as men's shirts, and both start from KSh 3,200.

Women's shirts are the same price band as men's shirts because the construction work is the same. The patterns are different, but the fabric, the thread, the buttons and the time on the bench are equivalent. We do not charge extra for smaller or larger sizes, and we do not charge extra for the pattern difference.

What a Made-to-Measure Shirt Fixes That Alteration Cannot

Alteration can improve a shirt that is already close to your size. Made-to-measure fixes the things alteration cannot reach, because it starts from your measurements rather than from a size chart.

  • Neck and collar. A made-to-measure collar is cut to your neck plus the correct allowance. No pulling, no gap, no slack.
  • Shoulder width. The shoulder seam sits exactly on your shoulder point. Not an inch wide, not an inch narrow.
  • Sleeve length and sleeve width. Cuff sits at the wrist bone. Sleeve width is correct for the arm without ballooning at the forearm.
  • Chest and waist balance. The shirt is not cut straight from chest to hem. It is cut to your chest, tapered to your waist, and eased back out at the hem so it stays tucked.
  • Shirt length. Long enough to stay tucked all day, not so long that it bunches at the hip. Correct length for the way you wear it.
  • Collar shape. Chosen to suit your face shape and the way you wear the shirt, with or without a tie.
  • Cuff style. Single button, double button, French, mitred or rounded. Correct cuff opening for your wrist and any watch you wear.
  • Detail choices. Pocket or no pocket, placket style, back yoke, monogram. All decided by you at the consultation.

What is the difference between made-to-measure and bespoke shirts?

A bespoke shirt is drafted from a paper pattern cut specifically for your body. A made-to-measure shirt starts from a base pattern that is adjusted to your measurements at the chest, waist, shoulder, sleeve and collar. Both give a significantly better fit than off-the-rack. Bespoke allows more freedom on details such as the collar shape, cuff style, placket width and button choice, and it takes longer. Made-to-measure is faster and slightly cheaper while still fitting properly.
